Good marketing begins with an honest conversation about goals, resources, and what can reasonably be measured.

What we aim to provide

  • ✓Thoughtful recommendations tailored to your book
  • ✓Clear scope, deliverables, and communication
  • ✓Attention to campaign data and useful indicators

What we won't promise

Book sales and advertising outcomes depend on many factors, including reader demand, genre, pricing, reviews, competition, and budget. We do not guarantee bestseller status, a specific number of sales, or a particular return on ad spend.

Any proposed services, fees, and expected deliverables should be agreed upon in writing before work begins.

FAQs

Questions authors often ask

A few helpful details before we start a conversation.

Who do you work with?

We focus on independent and self-published authors. Share your book details and goals so we can discuss whether the available services fit your needs.

Can you help if my book is already published?

Yes. Listing reviews, promotional planning, and advertising support may be relevant to an existing title as well as a new release.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the work involved. Contact us with your book details and goals, and ask for a written outline of the scope, fees, and deliverables before committing.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. Marketing can help improve discoverability and provide useful performance data, but sales are affected by factors outside any marketer's control.

What should I include in my first message?

Your book title, genre, retail or author-page link if available, whether it is published, and your main marketing goal are a great start. Please don't send passwords or payment information.
